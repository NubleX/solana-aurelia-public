04 · Steady flow

A measured release over year one

Community distribution and liquidity depth increase gradually. Reserved allocations are not treated as immediate circulating supply.

The public principle is simple: currency enters circulation progressively, liquidity grows with it, and no large scheduled release is designed to create a sudden price shock.
05 · Tradeoffs

What Classic SPL cannot do - and what you keep instead.

Cannot do on-chain

  • No max-wallet hook that rejects oversized balances
  • No transfer tax / auto-burn on every swap
  • No clawback after freeze is revoked
  • No token-level vesting on the team bucket

Keep instead

  • Full DEX and wallet coverage
  • Hard cap that is actually hard (mint gone)
  • No developer freeze button - verifiable on-chain
  • Soft anti-whale at launch via LP sizing + culture + DAO

Token-2022 is parked, not promised. A later v2 only if DEX support is no longer the bottleneck.
